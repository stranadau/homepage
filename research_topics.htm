Research Topics

    • (1) Modal behaviors of a Timoshenko beam with an edge crack
      We develop analytical and semi-analytical formulations to quantify crack-induced changes in modal characteristics of beam-like waveguides, particularly when shear deformation and rotary inertia require a Timoshenko-type description. A recurring emphasis is mechanistic interpretability: explicit frequency equations and coupling mechanisms that explain when and why modal sensitivity increases, supporting both forward prediction and inversion-ready modeling.
      • Closed-form / reduced frequency equations for cracked beams under general supports
        • A closed-form frequency equation of an arbitrarily supported beam with a transverse open crack considering axial–bending modal coupling , Journal of Sound and Vibration, 2020.
        • Generic frequency equation for a cracked beam based on the phase closure principle , Journal of Sound and Vibration, 2022.
        • A simplified frequency equation for a beam with an open crack from a wave perspective , Wave Motion, 2017.
      • Deep-beam crack modal behavior (axial–bending–shear coupling; mixed crack-mode effects)
        • Investigating the modal behaviors of a deep beam with a transverse open crack , Journal of Sound and Vibration, 2024.
      • High-frequency regime and “node-crack insensitivity” revisited
        • Investigating a common premise in SHM: Are higher modal frequencies more sensitive to an incipient crack on a beam than lower ones? , Engineering Structures, 2018.
        • Investigating the modal behaviors of a beam with a transverse crack on a high-frequency bending node , International Journal of Mechanical Sciences, 2022.
    • (2) Physics-informed machine learning for engineering structures
      We develop physics-informed learning pipelines for structural mechanics problems where governing equations are known but parameter estimation and prediction can be ill-posed under sparse/noisy measurements. The focus is on embedding physics (constraints from mechanics and evolution laws) into learning to obtain data-efficient, physically consistent, and engineering-usable models for prediction and state/parameter inference.
      • Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) for time-dependent behavior of prestressed concrete (PSC) members
        • Predicting the Early-Age Time-Dependent Behaviors of a Prestressed Concrete Beam by Using Physics-Informed Neural Network , Sensors, 2023.
      • Integral-equation-driven estimation as a bridge between mechanics and learning/identification
        • Finite difference equation of Volterra integral equation for estimating the time-dependent prestress loss of a prestressed concrete beam , Structures, 2024.
      • ML-assisted crack assessment via physics-based feature construction and sensing augmentation
        • Crack Assessment of Beam Using Machine Learning with Augmented Sensing, (book chapter listed on the profile), 2023.
    • (3) Guided wave propagation using surface-bonded piezoelectric wafers
      This theme investigates guided-wave excitation, propagation, scattering, and wavefield manipulation using surface-bonded piezoelectric (PZT) wafers/transducers for active SHM. We emphasize physics-based interpretation of wave/electromechanical signals, time-reversal acoustics, baseline-independent diagnostics, and efficient coupled PZT–structure modeling.
      • Time-reversal guided waves (Lamb waves)
        • Physical understanding of time reversal in Lamb-wave propagation
          • Understanding a time reversal process in Lamb wave propagation , Wave Motion, 2009.
        • Time-reversal active sensing for composite plates (damage detection)
          • Time reversal active sensing for health monitoring of a composite plate , Journal of Sound and Vibration, 2007.
          • Damage detection in composite plates by using an enhanced time reversal method , Journal of Aerospace Engineering (ASCE), 2007.
        • Spatial focusing of A0 Lamb-wave mode (numerical simulation and performance evaluation)
          • Simulating a time reversal process for A0 Lamb wave mode on a rectangular plate using a virtual sensor array model , Transaction of Korean Society of Noise and Vibration Engineering, 2010.
          • Investigating the spatial focusing performance of time reversal Lamb waves on a plate through the finite element method , Transaction of Korean Society of Noise and Vibration Engineering, 2011.
          • Numerical simulation and investigation of the spatial focusing of time reversal A0 Lamb wave mode using circular piezoelectric transducers collocated on a rectangular plate , Journal of Sound and Vibration, 2013.
      • Reference-free damage diagnosis with PZT wafers (impedance / electromechanical signals)
        • Reference-free impedance-based crack detection in plates
          • Reference-free impedance-based crack detection in plates , Journal of Sound and Vibration, 2011.
        • Reference-free diagnosis using electromechanical signals from collocated PZT wafers
          • Investigating electro-mechanical signals from collocated piezoelectric wafers for the reference-free damage diagnosis of a plate , Smart Materials & Structures, 2011.
      • Efficient coupled PZT–structure modeling (broadband simulation / spectral elements)
        • Spectral element formulation for coupled PZT wafer–beam dynamics
          • Spectral element formulation for dynamic analysis of a coupled piezoelectric wafer and beam system , Computers & Structures, 2010.
      • Ultrasonic guided modes for defect imaging and quantification (pipes)
        • Defect visualization and quantification using longitudinal ultrasonic modes
          • Pipe Defect Visualization and Quantification Using Longitudinal Ultrasonic Modes , International Journal of Structural Stability and Dynamics, 2014.
      • Extension to cement-based materials (multi-mode PZT sensing)
        • In-situ profiling of evolving dynamic properties in cement-based materials
          • Multi-mode piezoelectric (PZT) sensing for in-situ profiling of the evolution of dynamic properties in cement-based materials , Cement and Concrete Composites, 2025.
    • (4) Inverse problems and system identification
      We develop numerically stable inverse-analysis and system-identification methodologies to infer hidden structural states and parameters from limited, noise-contaminated measurements. Emphasis is placed on regularization design (Tikhonov, sparsity-promoting, and spatial regularization), robust hyperparameter selection, and practical inverse pipelines such as acceleration-to-displacement reconstruction for low-frequency dominant structures.
      • Displacement reconstruction from acceleration
        • FIR-filter design for drift-robust displacement recovery in low-frequency dominant structures
          • Hae Sung Lee, Yun Hwa Hong and Hyun Woo Park, Design of an FIR filter for the displacement reconstruction using measured acceleration in low-frequency dominant structures , International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, Vol. 82, No. 4, 403–434, 2010.
      • Regularization-aware system identification
        • 1-norm regularization for discontinuous / localized system parameters
          • Hyun Woo Park, Man Woo Park, Byeong Kyu Ahn and Hae Sung Lee, 1-norm based regularization scheme for system identification of structures with discontinuous system parameters , International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, Vol. 69, No. 3, 504–523, 2007.
        • Optimal selection of the Tikhonov regularization factor for linear elastic continua
          • Hyun Woo Park, Soobong Shin and Hae Sung Lee, Determination of an optimal regularization factor in system identification with Tikhonov regularization for linear elastic continua , International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, Vol. 51, No. 10, 1211–1230, 2001.
      • Inverse identification of inclusions (geometry and material properties)
        • Boundary parameterization for simultaneous identification of inclusion shape and material properties
          • Hae Sung Lee, Cheon Jong Park and Hyun Woo Park, Identification of geometric shapes and material properties of inclusions in two-dimensional finite bodies by boundary parameterization , Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering, Vol. 181, No. 1–2, 1–20, 2000.
        • Spatial regularization for stable identification of inclusion geometry
          • Hae Sung Lee, Yong Han Kim, Cheon Jong Park and Hyun Woo Park, A new spatial regularization scheme for the identification of the geometric shape of an inclusion in a finite body , International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, Vol. 46, No. 7, 973–992, 1999.
