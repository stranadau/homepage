Research Topics

  • (1) Modal behaviors of a Timoshenko beam with an edge crack
    We develop analytical / semi-analytical vibration models for cracked beam-like waveguides where shear deformation and rotary inertia are non-negligible. The emphasis is on interpretable frequency equations and coupling mechanisms (axial–bending and axial–bending–shear) that explain when and why modal sensitivity increases.
    • Deep-beam crack dynamics: for low aspect-ratio beams, mixed crack-mode effects can induce combined axial–bending–shear behavior near the crack, requiring coupled formulations rather than pure bending approximations.
    • Frequency-equation formulations: closed-form or reduced iterative equations are constructed to support rapid parametric studies and inversion-ready modeling under arbitrary support conditions.
    • High-frequency regime: we clarify limitations of “node-crack insensitivity” assumptions and quantify when higher modes provide stronger crack signatures.
    • Key papers: JSV 2024 (deep beam with transverse open crack), JSV 2020 (closed-form frequency equation with axial–bending coupling), Eng. Struct. 2018 (revisiting “higher-mode sensitivity” premise), IJMS 2022 (crack on a high-frequency bending node), Wave Motion 2017 (simplified frequency equation from a wave perspective), JSV 2022 (phase-closure based generic frequency equation)
  • (2) Physics-informed machine learning for engineering structures
    We design physics-informed learning workflows for time-dependent structural responses where governing equations are known but conventional calibration can be ill-posed under sparse/noisy measurements. The goal is data-efficient and physically consistent prediction/estimation suitable for engineering interpretation and decision-making.
    • PINN for time-dependent PSC behavior: physics constraints are embedded to learn coupled evolution of prestress and material/time-dependent mechanisms without relying solely on dense time discretization.
    • Engineering usability: emphasis on stable training, mechanistic interpretability, and model forms that can be integrated into SHM pipelines (state tracking and parameter inference).
    • Key papers: Sensors 2023 (PINN for early-age time-dependent behaviors of PSC beams), Structures 2024 (Volterra-integral-equation-based prestress-loss estimation)
  • (3) Guided wave propagation using surface-bonded piezoelectric wafers
    This theme investigates guided-wave excitation, propagation, and wavefield manipulation using surface-bonded piezoelectric (PZT) wafers/transducers for active SHM. We emphasize physics-based interpretation of measured electromechanical and wave signals, including scattering, mode selectivity, and robustness against baseline uncertainty. Key methodological pillars include time-reversal acoustics, reference-free PZT/impedance diagnostics, and computationally efficient wave solvers (e.g., spectral element formulations).
    • Time-reversal guided waves (Lamb waves): numerical/experimental investigations of time-reversal focusing in plates, including A0-mode spatial focusing using collocated circular PZT transducers and virtual sensor-array modeling; studies also clarify the underlying physics of the time-reversal process in Lamb wave propagation.
    • Reference-free damage diagnosis with PZT wafers: impedance/electromechanical-signal features enabling crack detection in plates without requiring a pristine baseline, improving practicality under operational/temperature variability.
    • Efficient coupled PZT–structure modeling: spectral element formulations for coupled piezoelectric wafer–beam dynamics, enabling broadband simulations and parametric studies relevant to actuator/sensor design and diagnostic sensitivity.
    • Ultrasonic guided modes for defect imaging: visualization and quantification of defects in pipes using longitudinal ultrasonic modes, connecting guided-wave physics to imaging/quantification workflows.
    • Key papers: JSV 2013 (time-reversal A0 focusing with circular PZT transducers), Wave Motion 2009 (physics of time reversal in Lamb waves), JSV 2007 (time-reversal active sensing for composite plates), ASCE J. Aerospace Eng. 2007 (enhanced time-reversal damage detection), JSV 2011 (reference-free impedance-based crack detection), Smart Mater. Struct. 2011 (reference-free diagnosis via electromechanical signals), Computers & Structures 2010 (spectral element for PZT wafer–beam coupling), IJSSD 2014 (pipe defect visualization using longitudinal modes), KSNVE 2010 (virtual sensor array for A0 time reversal), KSNVE 2011 (FE study of time-reversal focusing performance)
  • (4) Inverse problems and system identification
    We develop numerically stable inverse-analysis and system-identification methodologies to infer hidden structural states and parameters from limited, noise-contaminated measurements. Emphasis is placed on regularization design (Tikhonov, sparsity-promoting, and spatial regularization), robust hyperparameter selection, and practical inverse pipelines such as acceleration-to-displacement reconstruction for low-frequency dominant civil structures.
    • Displacement reconstruction from acceleration: FIR-filter design and regularized formulations that enable stable displacement recovery in low-frequency dominant structures, mitigating drift/noise amplification typical of direct numerical integration.
    • Regularization-aware system identification: identification under ill-posedness using Tikhonov regularization with principled selection of the regularization factor, and 1-norm regularization to recover discontinuous or localized parameter variations without excessive smoothing.
    • Inverse identification of inclusions: boundary-parameterization and spatial-regularization schemes for simultaneous estimation of inclusion geometry and material properties in finite bodies, addressing non-uniqueness and sensitivity to measurement errors.
    • Key papers: IJNME 2010 (FIR filter for displacement reconstruction), IJNME 2007 (1-norm regularization for discontinuous parameters), IJNME 2001 (optimal Tikhonov factor in system identification), CMAME 2000 (boundary parameterization for inclusion ID), IJNME 1999 (spatial regularization for inclusion shape ID)
