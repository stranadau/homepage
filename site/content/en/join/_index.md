## What we study

- **Theory of crack diagnosis**: closed-form solutions for how waves are reflected and transmitted at a crack in a beam.
- **Piezoelectric sensing experiments**: coin-sized PZT wafers bonded on structures send waves and detect damage from the returning signals.
- **AI + mechanics**: physics-informed machine learning that predicts cracks and structural behavior from limited data.
- **Real bridges (PSC girders)**: estimating prestress and camber of bridge girders from field measurements.

See the [Research](../research/) page for details.

## What you will learn

- Structural dynamics, wave mechanics and inverse analysis
- Analysis and experiments with MATLAB, ABAQUS and LabVIEW
- Piezoelectric sensing and signal processing
- Physics-informed machine learning
- Writing international journal papers and presenting at conferences
